        About this demo

        This is a free prototype that shows what a custom app could do for the transportation team. It runs entirely in this browser. All names, places, events and numbers are invented.

        The full version adds:

        • Department tabs: transportation, food and beverage, events, and sport
        • Offline use with automatic sync when the connection returns
        • Manager logins with approvals and ride assignment to drivers
        • Beverage orders
        • Tables
        • Contacts
