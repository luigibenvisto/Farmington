      About this demo

      This is a free prototype that shows what a custom app could do for the transportation team. All drivers, events and numbers are invented. When live sync is set up, phones share this invented data through a free test database; otherwise it stays in this browser.

      The full version adds:

      • Department tabs: transportation, food and beverage, events, and sport
      • Offline use with automatic sync when the connection returns
      • Manager logins with approvals and ride assignment to drivers
      • Beverage orders
      • Tables
      • Contacts
